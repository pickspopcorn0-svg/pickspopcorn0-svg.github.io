Indian Cinema

Indian cinema worth your time.

Indian cinema is one of the most varied film industries in the world and one of the most misunderstood outside it. The Bollywood label gets applied to everything, but the actual output spans multiple industries across different languages with completely different sensibilities. South Indian cinema in particular has been producing some of the most ambitious filmmaking anywhere for the last decade. RRR is an action epic with genuine emotional scale. Andhadhun is a thriller that plays with moral logic better than most Hollywood films manage. Sardar Udham is a restrained, slow-burn political biography that demands patience and rewards it. The collection here covers that range: action, drama, comedy, historical, thriller, and the mythic epic. If you are new to Indian cinema, the intro editorial is a good place to start. If you already know the space, the full-throttle piece covers the films that go hardest.
