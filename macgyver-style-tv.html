For People Who Miss MacGyver-Style TV Shows

This editorial is for people who still enjoy shows driven by ingenuity, calm problem-solving, and competence.

Below is a small, carefully chosen set of series that carry that MacGyver-style energy. Different tones, different settings, same core idea: problems are meant to be solved.

Popcorn Picks

Scorpion

2014-2018 • 4 seasons

Scorpion takes the MacGyver formula and cranks it up to a whole team of geniuses.

If you miss that rhythm of setup → improvisation → solution, Scorpion delivers it consistently.

White Collar

2009-2014 • 6 seasons

White Collar sits comfortably in that MacGyver-like sweet spot: smart but fun.

Burn Notice

2007-2013 • 7 seasons

Burn Notice is probably the purest hit of MacGyver DNA on this list.

Instead of killing tension, that clarity sharpens it.

Chuck

2007-2012 • 5 seasons

MacGyver-style thinking wrapped in heart and humor.

Limitless

2015-2016 • 1 season

The simple pleasure of watching smart solutions unfold.
