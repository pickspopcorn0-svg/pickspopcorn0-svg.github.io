Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Popcorn Picks (“we”, “our”, “us”) handles information when you visit our website, including how cookies may be used for analytics and advertising, and the choices you have.

Effective date: 8 February 2026
Applies to: popcornpicks.in

1) Who We Are

Popcorn Picks is a curated discovery website for films and series across the world. We do not run user accounts and we do not require you to provide personal information to browse the site.

2) Information We Collect

Information you provide: We do not offer account creation. If you contact us by email, you share information voluntarily (such as your email address and message content).

Information collected automatically: Like most websites, limited information may be processed automatically when you visit, such as:

  • Device and browser information (e.g., device type, browser type, operating system)
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and general site interaction data
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address (city/region-level, not precise GPS)
  • General diagnostics (e.g., load times, errors) to improve performance
We aim to keep data collection minimal. Where analytics or advertising cookies are involved, we rely on your consent choices via the cookie banner and cookie settings.

3) Cookies & Your Choices

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use cookies and similar technologies for two categories:

  • Analytics cookies (optional): help us understand how people use the site and what to improve.
  • Advertising cookies (optional): used by advertising partners to measure and/or personalize ads.

When you first visit Popcorn Picks, you can choose to Accept, Reject, or Manage cookies. You can change your choices at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of the site.

If you reject or disable ads cookies, ads may still be shown, but they may be less relevant (non-personalized).

4) Analytics (Google Analytics)

If you allow analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use Popcorn Picks. This helps us improve pages, navigation, and content quality.

If you do not allow analytics cookies, analytics storage remains disabled and Google Analytics will not store analytics cookies for measurement.

5) Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display ads through Google AdSense and/or Google’s advertising partners. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

Depending on your consent settings, ads may be:

  • Personalized (based on interests inferred from browsing behavior across sites/apps), or
  • Non-personalized (based on contextual signals like page content, general location, or device type)

You can also manage ad personalization through your Google account settings. (For example, Google “Ads Settings”.)

Important: Popcorn Picks does not directly receive your Google account details. Ad serving and personalization (if enabled) is handled by Google and its partners.

6) Legal Basis for Processing (General)

Where applicable, we rely on:

  • Consent for analytics and advertising cookies (you choose via the cookie banner/settings).
  • Legitimate interests for basic site operation, security, and performance (e.g., preventing abuse, keeping the site stable).

7) Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Email messages you send us may be retained to respond and for record-keeping.
  • Analytics/ads data retention is controlled primarily by the relevant provider settings (e.g., Google) and your consent choices.

8) Third-Party Links

Popcorn Picks may link to third-party sites (for example, streaming services, external references, or social platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Please review their policies directly.

9) Children’s Privacy

Popcorn Picks is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us via email, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the site and any information we receive. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Please use the site at your own discretion and avoid sharing sensitive personal information by email.

11) Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights over your personal information (for example: access, correction, deletion, or objection). Since Popcorn Picks does not run user accounts, most requests relate to email communications or cookie preferences.

  • Cookie choices: Use “Cookie settings” in the footer to update analytics/ads consent.
  • Email requests: Contact us if you want us to delete or update an email conversation.

12) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the site after changes means you understand the updated policy.
