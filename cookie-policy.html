This Cookie Policy explains how Popcorn Picks ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at popcornpicks.in (the "Website"). By using our Website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies serve various purposes, including remembering your preferences, analyzing how you use the site, and delivering relevant content and advertisements.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas of the Website.

Examples:

  • Session cookies that maintain your browsing session
  • Security cookies to prevent fraudulent activity
  • Load balancing cookies to distribute traffic efficiently

Duration: Session-based (deleted when you close your browser)

2.2 Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our Website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve the Website's functionality and user experience.

We may use:

  • Google Analytics to analyze visitor behavior and traffic patterns
  • Performance monitoring tools to identify technical issues

Information collected includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Links clicked and navigation patterns
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referral source (how you found our Website)

Duration: Typically up to 2 years

2.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow the Website to remember choices you make (such as your preferred language or region) and provide enhanced, personalized features.

Examples:

  • Remembering your display preferences (theme, layout)
  • Storing your watchlist or saved content
  • Remembering login information (if applicable)

Duration: Varies, typically 30 days to 1 year

2.4 Advertising and Targeting Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. They may be set by our advertising partners through our Website.

We may use:

  • Google AdSense for displaying contextual advertisements

These cookies may:

  • Track your browsing activity across different websites
  • Build a profile of your interests
  • Limit the number of times you see an advertisement
  • Measure advertising campaign effectiveness

Duration: Typically up to 1-2 years

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View what cookies have been set and delete them individually or all at once
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies from being set
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

How to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies

5.2 Opt-Out Tools

You can also use the following tools to control advertising cookies:

  • Google Ads Settings: Opt out of personalized Google ads
  • Your Online Choices: Manage preferences across multiple advertisers
  • Network Advertising Initiative: Opt out of behavioral advertising

Important Note: Blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on our Website. Some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the Website to operate.

6. Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want your online activity tracked. Currently, there is no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals. We do not currently respond to DNT signals, but we respect your privacy choices and provide you with tools to control cookies as described above.

7. Mobile Devices

Mobile devices may have different settings for managing cookies and tracking. Check your device settings or privacy controls for options related to:

  • Limiting ad tracking
  • Resetting your advertising identifier
  • Managing app permissions

8. Cookie Duration

Cookies can be either "session cookies" or "persistent cookies":

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. Duration varies from days to years depending on the cookie type and purpose.
