Best Chinese Fantasy Dramas

If you are new to C drama fantasy, here is the easiest way to think about it. Xianxia is gods, immortals, reincarnation, realms, and big destiny love stories. Wuxia fantasy is the martial world, sect rivalries, reputation, and people trying to stay human while carrying a sword. Cultivation is growth, training, power levels, and earning your place the hard way.

These picks are here because they have a clear vibe. You will know what you are signing up for, and why it is worth it.

Listening Snow Tower

2019 • 56 episodes
Wuxia fantasyMartial worldRomanceTragedy vibes

Imagine a classic wuxia world with a darker, moody feel. This drama is full of sword fights, secret sects, and a slow romance running quietly in the background. The tone stays serious and a little tragic, and the atmosphere really pulls you into its martial world.

There are tender moments between the leads, but the story mostly stays intense and conflict-driven. Love and duty keep clashing while battles never really stop. If you like something heroic, heavy, and slightly heartbreaking, this one fits that old-school wuxia mood well.

Best for: fans of classic wuxia stories and brooding heroes.
Not for: viewers looking for something light, cheerful, or fast-paced.

Main cast: Qin Junjie, Yuan Bingyan
Best for: people who like a colder, moodier martial world story.
Not for: viewers who want bright comedy and clean happy endings.

Ancient Love Poetry

2021 • 49 episodes
XianxiaGodsLong time loveFate and sacrifice

This is a big immortal love story with gods, goddesses, and a romance that stretches across a very long time. The world is fantasy-heavy, with beautiful realms and a strong focus on destiny. At its heart, it’s really about two people who keep getting pulled apart and finding their way back to each other.

It does take a few episodes to settle in because the story spends time setting up the world. But once the emotions start hitting, they hit hard. The romance slowly builds from sweet moments into real heartbreak, and it’s easy to get very invested in the lead couple.

Best for: viewers who enjoy emotional xianxia romances with fate, sacrifice, and strong devotion.
Not for: people who want fast pacing or realistic storytelling instead of fantasy-heavy drama.

Main cast: Zhou Dongyu, Xu Kai, Liu Xueyi
Best for: xianxia fans who want devotion and consequences.
Not for: viewers who hate destiny romance and long separations.

Who Rules the World

2022 • 40 episodes
WuxiaPoliticsPower gamesPartners

This is a fun wuxia adventure with plenty of action and a really enjoyable romance. Both leads are skilled fighters, confident, and sharp, so watching them challenge each other is half the fun. The story starts on a lighter note, with playful banter and a bit of flirting as they travel through the martial world.

As things move forward, the plot brings in politics, power struggles, and real stakes. What works well is that the romance never overshadows the story. The leads feel like true partners who trust each other in fights and tough decisions, not just in quiet moments. The balance between action, strategy, and chemistry keeps it easy to watch and hard to drop.

Best for: viewers who enjoy strong lead pairs, smart romance, and steady pacing.
Not for: people looking for heavy magic, intense angst, or very dark fantasy themes.

Main cast: Yang Yang, Zhao Lusi
Best for: viewers who like smart leads and clean pacing.
Not for: people who only want pure fantasy magic and realms.

Love and Redemption

2020 • 59 episodes
XianxiaPast livesBig angstDevotion

This is one of those xianxia romances that really leans into emotions. The story follows a love that stretches across multiple lifetimes, and nothing comes easy for the leads. You’ll get sweet moments early on, but they don’t last long before misunderstandings and pain start piling up.

The overall mood stays intense and emotional, especially as past-life secrets slowly come out. What pulls you in is how much the characters go through for each other. Their relationship feels hard-earned, and by the time things start making sense, you’re already deeply invested. It’s beautiful in its own way, but it definitely asks you to sit through a lot before giving any relief.

Best for: viewers who enjoy emotional xianxia romances with devotion, sacrifice, and heavy feelings.
Not for: people who dislike angst, misunderstandings, or long stretches of emotional suffering.

Main cast: Yuan Bingyan, Cheng Yi
Best for: viewers who live for devotion and emotional chaos.
Not for: people who get tired of repeated trials and misunderstandings.

Ashes of Love

2018 • 63 episodes
XianxiaImmortal realmsLove triangleSlow burn into pain

This drama starts out surprisingly light and sweet. The female lead is innocent and clueless about love, which leads to a lot of cute and funny moments early on. The romance feels soft at first, almost like a fairytale, and it’s easy to relax into it.

Then the story slowly turns serious. Emotions get heavier, misunderstandings grow, and things start to hurt in a real way. By the middle of the show, it becomes very emotional, with sacrifices and choices that actually change the characters. It’s the kind of drama that pulls you in slowly and then refuses to let go.

Best for: viewers who enjoy emotional fantasy romances that start sweet and turn intense.
Not for: people who dislike love triangles, misunderstandings, or heavy emotional drama.

Main cast: Yang Zi, Deng Lun, Luo Yunxi
Best for: people who enjoy romance that eventually bites back.
Not for: viewers who want a lighter, calmer xianxia mood.

Battle Through the Heavens

2018 • 45 episodes
CultivationTraining arcRivalriesLevel up story

This one is less about romance and more about growth and comeback. The story follows a young man who loses everything early on and has to rebuild himself from scratch. It’s very focused on training, power progression, and proving people wrong.

The pacing stays energetic, with constant challenges, rivalries, and fight scenes. Watching the main character slowly get stronger is genuinely satisfying. There is some friendship and light romance along the way, but the main focus is always on his journey forward. It feels like a classic underdog story set in a fantasy world.

Best for: viewers who enjoy cultivation stories, training arcs, and steady power growth.
Not for: people looking mainly for romance or emotional drama.

Main cast: Wu Lei (Leo Wu)
Best for: viewers who love training, power growth, and clear milestones.
Not for: people who only want romance and soft character drama.

The Untamed

2019 • 50 episodes
CultivationMysteryLoyaltyMoral conflict

This drama became huge for a reason. At its core, it’s about the bond between two very different people thrown into a world of clans, rules, and supernatural cases. One is playful and reckless, the other quiet and disciplined, and their dynamic is honestly one of the most enjoyable parts early on. Their interactions feel natural and warm, even when the story is dealing with dark mysteries.

As the plot moves forward, things get heavier. Secrets come out, loyalties are tested, and the consequences hit hard. The emotional weight builds slowly, and by the time it reaches the turning points, it’s hard not to feel deeply attached to the characters. It’s not just about magic or fights, but about standing by someone when the whole world turns against them.

Best for: viewers who enjoy strong character bonds, slow emotional buildup, and detailed fantasy worlds.
Not for: people who want a fast, simple story or a clear romantic focus.

Main cast: Xiao Zhan, Wang Yibo
Best for: viewers who like mystery plus character bonds plus a big world.
Not for: people who want instant payoff in the first episode.
If you want a lighter, stylish entry point: Who Rules the World.
If you want maximum xianxia emotion: Love and Redemption or Ashes of Love.
If you want a cult favorite that turns into an obsession: The Untamed.
