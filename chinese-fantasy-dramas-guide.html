Best Chinese Fantasy Dramas You Should Watch

If you’re new to C-drama fantasy, here’s the map: xianxia is gods, immortals, realms, reincarnation — and love stories that stretch across lifetimes. wuxia fantasy is the martial world: sect rivalries, reputation, blades, and people trying to stay human. cultivation is the grind: training, breakthroughs, power levels that actually mean something.

These picks are chosen for clarity — you’ll know the vibe before episode one.

China Fantasy 7 Picks Popcorn Picks
Start with clarity, not hype. A good first pick explains the genre while you’re watching it.
Popcorn Picks
Pick 01 · Wuxia Fantasy
Listening Snow Tower
2019 · 56 episodes
Martial worldRomanceTragedy vibesCold tone

This is wuxia for when you want something cold and serious. The martial world here is not fun. Sects remember grudges forever. People smile at you while planning your downfall. Trust is rare and usually gets punished. The tone stays heavy from the beginning, and if that is what you are looking for, this delivers.

The action is frequent but it always feels like it matters. Fights are not just there to look cool. They break relationships. They settle old scores. They change the balance of power. The story keeps reminding you that being the strongest fighter is not enough if your reputation is destroyed or your allies turn on you.

The romance exists but it does not take over the show. It runs quietly underneath everything like a sad song you can barely hear. That actually works better for this kind of story.

Watch it when you want a martial world drama with a moody atmosphere and a romance that feels like it costs something just to exist.

Main cast: Qin Junjie, Yuan Bingyan
Best for: people who want a colder, moodier wuxia story
Not for: viewers who need happy endings and bright energy
Pick 02 · Xianxia
Ancient Love Poetry
2021 · 49 episodes
GodsReincarnationFate & sacrificeLong-time love

Ancient Love Poetry is full xianxia. Gods, multiple realms, a love story that spans lifetimes, and a relationship that gets tested repeatedly before anyone is allowed to be happy. The first few episodes spend time building the world, so you need some patience, but once the emotional part kicks in it does not stop.

What makes this different from lighter xianxia is that love here has real consequences. When characters choose each other, something breaks somewhere else. Sacrifices are not symbolic. They actually hurt and change the relationship going forward.

Pick this when you want xianxia that goes big emotionally and makes you feel the cost of love instead of just showing you the pretty parts.

Main cast: Zhou Dongyu, Xu Kai, Liu Xueyi
Best for: xianxia fans who want devotion tested hard
Not for: viewers who hate destiny plots and long separations
Pick 03 · Wuxia
Who Rules the World
2022 · 40 episodes
PoliticsPower gamesPartnersEasy entry

Who Rules the World is probably the easiest wuxia to start with if you are new to the genre. It gives you action, romance, and political intrigue without making you choose which one matters more. Both leads are capable. Neither is dead weight.

The early episodes are lighter and more fun. By the time the politics and power struggles take over, you already believe in the relationship enough that it does not feel forced when things get serious.

Watch it if you want capable leads, clean pacing, and wuxia politics without drowning in heavy mythology.

Main cast: Yang Yang, Zhao Lusi
Best for: viewers who want capable leads and clean pacing
Not for: people who only want deep fantasy magic and realm hopping
Pick 04 · Xianxia
Love and Redemption
2020 · 59 episodes
Past livesBig angstDevotionTrials

This is the show to watch when you want xianxia that hurts on purpose and does not apologize for it. The early episodes are almost sweet. But that is a setup. Once it starts breaking things, it does not stop for a long time.

Secrets pile up. Past lives matter. Misunderstandings last for entire arcs. The devotion has to be proved through loss instead of just words.

Pick this when you want xianxia that earns its payoff by making you suffer first.

Main cast: Yuan Bingyan, Cheng Yi
Best for: viewers who want devotion tested until it breaks or proves itself
Not for: people who get tired of repeated trials and slow payoffs
Pick 05 · Xianxia
Ashes of Love
2018 · 63 episodes
Immortal realmsLove triangleSlow burnConsequences

Ashes of Love earns its reputation by making you care before it makes you hurt. The opening is soft, almost fairytale-like. That warmth is a trap — and the show knows exactly what it is doing.

The shift is gradual and the emotions stick. By the heavy middle section you’re already too attached to walk away.

Watch it when you want romance that starts like a dream and ends somewhere darker — with regret that actually stays.

Main cast: Yang Zi, Deng Lun, Luo Yunxi
Best for: people who want romance that starts soft then bites back
Not for: viewers who want light xianxia from start to finish
Pick 06 · Cultivation
Battle Through the Heavens
2018 · 45 episodes
Training arcRivalriesLevel upUnderdog

If you want a cultivation story about grinding your way back from rock bottom, this is it. The main character loses everything early — power, status, respect — and rebuilds from scratch. The progress feels visible, and that’s the point.

This isn’t a romance drama or a character study. The satisfaction comes from watching someone earn power through effort instead of luck.

Watch it when you want cultivation with a clear underdog climb and constant forward motion.

Main cast: Wu Lei (Leo Wu)
Best for: viewers who love training arcs, rivals, and visible progress
Not for: people looking for romance or deep character drama
Pick 07 · Cultivation
The Untamed
2019 · 50 episodes
MysteryLoyaltyMoral conflictClan politics

The Untamed became huge for a reason. It gets the central bond right: chaotic vs disciplined, reckless vs controlled — and the dynamic holds even when the plot gets dark.

The structure leans mystery and supernatural cases, not just fights and power levels. The moral questions sharpen as it goes: what happens when doing the right thing makes you the enemy?

Watch it when you want cultivation with mystery, loyalty, and a story that grows heavier without losing what made you care.

Main cast: Xiao Zhan, Wang Yibo
Best for: viewers who want mystery and loyalty alongside cultivation
Not for: people who need instant payoff and hate slow builds
Mini Map (Optional)
Wuxia / 武侠
Martial heroes in the “jianghu” world — training, honor, sect rivalries, politics under the surface.
Xianxia / 仙侠
Immortal fantasy — realms, tribulations, reincarnation, gods & demons, love across lifetimes.
Cultivation / 修炼
Leveling up through training and breakthroughs. Power stages matter and progress is the hook.
If you want a lighter, accessible entry point: Who Rules the World.
If you want maximum xianxia emotion and angst: Love and Redemption or Ashes of Love.
If you want a cult favorite that turns into an obsession: The Untamed.
