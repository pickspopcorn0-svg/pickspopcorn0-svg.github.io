Best Chinese Fantasy Dramas

If you are new to C drama fantasy, here is the easiest way to think about it. Xianxia is gods, immortals, reincarnation, realms, and big destiny love stories. Wuxia fantasy is the martial world, sect rivalries, reputation, and people trying to stay human while carrying a sword. Cultivation is growth, training, power levels, and earning your place the hard way.

These picks are here because they have a clear vibe. You will know what you are signing up for, and why it is worth it.

Listening Snow Tower

2019 • 56 episodes
Wuxia fantasyMartial worldRomanceTragedy vibes

The hook is simple: the leader of a feared organization is tired. Not weak, not clueless. Just exhausted from living inside constant conflict. Enemies never stop circling, allies always have conditions, and peace is something you borrow, not something you own.

Then the story brings in a second lead with her own history and her own sharp edges. Their connection is not cute and fluffy. It feels like two people trying to breathe in a world that refuses to give them air.

Watch it for atmosphere and that classic wuxia feeling where loyalty has a price and love is never simple.

Main cast: Qin Junjie, Yuan Bingyan
Best for: people who like a colder, moodier martial world story.
Not for: viewers who want bright comedy and clean happy endings.

Ancient Love Poetry

2021 • 49 episodes
XianxiaGodsLong time loveFate and sacrifice

This is the kind of xianxia that starts with divine status but quickly turns into something more personal. A young true god grows up fast, because responsibility comes early and it comes heavy.

The romance is not just chemistry. It is timing, duty, separation, and the painful logic of immortals who have centuries to regret one decision. When it hits, it hits because the story keeps asking the same question in different forms: what do you give up when you choose love, and what do you lose when you choose duty.

Watch it if you like myth scale emotions, big visuals, and that slow ache of people missing each other across time.

Main cast: Zhou Dongyu, Xu Kai, Liu Xueyi
Best for: xianxia fans who want devotion and consequences.
Not for: viewers who hate destiny romance and long separations.

Who Rules the World

2022 • 40 episodes
WuxiaPoliticsPower gamesPartners

Two legends in the martial world keep colliding, then teaming up, then colliding again. On the surface it is playful: two equals, both clever, both deadly, both refusing to bow.

But the real story is what sits underneath. Hidden identities, court politics, and the kind of pressure where you can win a fight and still lose the bigger war. The romance works because it grows through trust earned in battle, not through random fate.

If you want something that balances action with strategy, this is a very easy recommendation.

Main cast: Yang Yang, Zhao Lusi
Best for: viewers who like smart leads and clean pacing.
Not for: people who only want pure fantasy magic and realms.

Love and Redemption

2020 • 59 episodes
XianxiaPast livesBig angstDevotion

This is full commitment melodrama xianxia, the kind where fate is cruel, love is stubborn, and misunderstandings do real damage. The female lead begins emotionally blocked, and the story uses that as a slow unlock for everything that follows.

The male lead is the engine of the drama. He keeps choosing her, again and again, even when it costs him. That is why people binge this. It is not subtle, it is not calm, but it knows exactly what it is doing: it wants you to suffer a little and then reward you.

Watch it when you want a long, addictive ride where feelings are loud and destiny keeps swinging a hammer.

Main cast: Yuan Bingyan, Cheng Yi
Best for: viewers who live for devotion and emotional chaos.
Not for: people who get tired of repeated trials and misunderstandings.

Ashes of Love

2018 • 63 episodes
XianxiaImmortal realmsLove triangleSlow burn into pain

It starts almost sweet. A sheltered girl steps into the wider world, meets powerful figures, and you think you are about to watch a classic fantasy romance.

Then it keeps turning. Jealousy shows up, pride shows up, misunderstandings pile up, and the story gets darker in a way that feels earned because you watched the slow mistakes happen.

The reason this drama has fans is simple: it is addictive. You keep saying one more episode because every choice creates a new problem, and the emotions keep escalating.

Main cast: Yang Zi, Deng Lun, Luo Yunxi
Best for: people who enjoy romance that eventually bites back.
Not for: viewers who want a lighter, calmer xianxia mood.

Battle Through the Heavens

2018 • 45 episodes
CultivationTraining arcRivalriesLevel up story

This one is for the part of your brain that loves progression. The lead falls from talent and status, and the story is basically him rebuilding from the ground up in a world where strength is currency.

It is not trying to be poetic. It is trying to be satisfying. New techniques, tougher opponents, bigger arenas, louder rivalries. You watch because you want the comeback, and you want the moment where people who looked down on him finally have to shut up.

If you like cultivation as a genre, this is a straightforward, bingeable pick.

Main cast: Wu Lei (Leo Wu)
Best for: viewers who love training, power growth, and clear milestones.
Not for: people who only want romance and soft character drama.

The Untamed

2019 • 50 episodes
CultivationMysteryLoyaltyMoral conflict

Imagine a world that keeps preaching righteousness while quietly protecting power. That is the mood of The Untamed. The lead takes a path the orthodox world fears, pays for it, disappears, then returns with old truths still buried.

What makes it hit is not just the fantasy. It is the bond at the center, the feeling of being judged by a system that never judged itself, and the slow unmasking of what really happened in the past.

Give it a few episodes to settle. Once the relationships and rules click, the story becomes hard to drop.

Main cast: Xiao Zhan, Wang Yibo
Best for: viewers who like mystery plus character bonds plus a big world.
Not for: people who want instant payoff in the first episode.
If you want a lighter, stylish entry point: Who Rules the World.
If you want maximum xianxia emotion: Love and Redemption or Ashes of Love.
If you want a cult favorite that turns into an obsession: The Untamed.
